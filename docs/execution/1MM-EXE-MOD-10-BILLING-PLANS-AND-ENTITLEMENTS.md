**What this document is for and when to open it.** It plans everything that takes money or changes what
a member is entitled to: the plans page, the pay-and-activate steps, the Black Badge payment, the billing
lifecycle BL1–BL7, store and Flutterwave billing, webhooks, refunds and disputes, the admin revenue
modules A19–A23 and the plans data the website shows. Open it before you touch `/v1/billing/*`,
`/v1/me/billing*`, `/webhooks/revenuecat`, `/webhooks/flutterwave`, `/admin/v1/billing/*`, the entitlement
matrix or `apps/web`.

## 1. Outcome

- **Member.** A verified member picks Premium or Gold on one plans page (D15), finishes only the steps
  that plan still needs, pays through the App Store, Google Play or (on the web) Flutterwave, and is
  activated at once; the action that sent them to D15 completes by itself [UF §9], [TR §2.1]. They can
  see their plan, history and receipts, switch or cancel, recover from a failed payment during a 7-day
  grace, choose what stays when a plan ends, and take one 3-day Premium trial [UF §10.2], [UF §15.15].
  The Black Badge payment is taken before review and refunded if the badge is not approved, except for
  fraud [TR §2.1], [OD-04].
- **Admin.** Finance sees every subscription and payment in USD across the three providers, reconciles
  them, fixes entitlement drift, replays webhooks, refunds under four-eyes approval, answers
  chargebacks, versions prices, edits the entitlement matrix under approval and reads revenue reports
  [ADM A19–A23].
- **Business.** One entitlement service is the only writer of grants and roles; access is never granted
  from a client claim, only after the provider confirms [TR §4.6], [TR §8.2]. The money ledger is kept
  7 years and aggregated in US cents [TR §16.1], [TR §4.6]. Offers stay built in the schema but off
  (`offers.enabled = false`) [OD-01].

### 4.3 Rules the API must enforce

1. Black Badge is a product: no subscription row, no renew, cancel or lapse path [TR §2.1].
2. Inside the apps every product is bought through the store; the web checkout endpoints refuse
   requests that are not from the web app [TR §4.6], [TR §8.2].
3. A webhook is verified, stored once, and the provider is re-queried before access is granted; a
   repeat of the same `(provider, event_id)` changes nothing [TR §8.2], [ADM A19].
4. Only the entitlement service writes `entitlement_grants`; roles change only through
   `refresh_member_role()`, which writes `role_history` [TR §4.6], [SQL].
5. One live subscription per member; `on_hold` counts, so a new purchase is refused while a store
   subscription is held [OD-37].
6. Premium and Gold need a verified member (Black Badge) [UF §15.13], [UF §15.14].
7. Upgrade is immediate and prorated; downgrade and cancel apply at period end with access kept
   until then [UF §10.2].
8. billedVia decides every action: store plans are changed and cancelled in the store; our API
   changes only Flutterwave plans; refunds follow rule 13 [TR §4.6].
9. Failed renewal: `billing.grace_days` of full access, reminders on days 1, 4 and 6, lapse on day 8
   and role recomputed [TR §4.6].
10. At lapse: verification stays; media above the new limit is hidden for `lapse.hide_extras_days`
    then deleted; the main photo always stays; conversations above the limit become read-only;
    Premium profile data is kept and restored on re-subscribe [UF §10.3].
11. If the member saves no BL6 choice, `billing.grace` applies defaults [TR §11]. The default order is
    not stated: **Recommendation** keep the lowest `position` media and the most recently active
    conversations.
12. Trial: Premium only, once per verified person keyed on `identity_key_hash`, length from the price
    (`trial_days`), N16 24 h before the charge [UF §10.2], [SQL].
13. Refunds: Google Play and Flutterwave are issued by us; App Store refunds are never issued by us,
    the member is guided to Apple and the refund is mirrored from the RevenueCat `REFUND` event with
    `reason = 'store_refund'` [OD-04], [ADM A20].
14. Every admin refund needs a second admin's approval while the threshold is 0; the requester cannot
    approve; the database refuses otherwise (`refund_approval_guard()`); system refunds are not gated
    [OD-05], [SQL].
15. A refund never exceeds the transaction amount minus refunds succeeded or pending [ADM A20].
16. Revenue is summed in US cents; local amounts are reference only; sandbox rows are labelled and
    left out of totals [TR §4.6], [ADM A19].
17. Prices are never edited in place: a change inserts version + 1 and closes the old row after
    approval [ADM A21].
18. A matrix change is visible in `GET /v1/me/entitlements` within one request [ADM A21].
19. `payment_methods` holds a token reference only; no PAN, CVV or full card data reaches our logs or
    database [DD §10], [TR §14].
20. Payment routes require `Idempotency-Key`; a replay returns the first response [TR §8.1].
21. GD2 accepts at most 5 countries, each `allows_international`; GD3 needs all four cards and is
    refreshed every `gold.briefing_refresh_months` (seeded 6) [TR §8.2], [SQL].
22. No offer can go live while `offers.enabled` is false [OD-01], [SQL `offer_campaign_guard()`].
23. A lost dispute only suggests a restriction (`source = 'chargeback'`); it is never automatic [ADM A20].

## 6. Web: website and billing web app

**Billing web app (`apps/web`)** [TR §6.2], [OD-15]. Sign-in is MOD-03's. This module adds, in order:
B9 Web (Black Badge pay), P5 / GD5 Web with the proration preview, BL1, BL2, BL3 for web-billed
members, change and cancel, update payment method. A store-billed member who signs in sees the plan
read-only with the store instructions. Payment options are card (including Verve), bank transfer and
USSD with 3-D Secure [TR §4.6]. States on every page: loading, empty, error, offline, restricted
(account-state guard from MOD-05). The app is gated by `payments.flutterwave_web`.

**Website (`apps/site`).** No checkout exists on the site: plan actions say "Get the app" [WEB W22].
This module serves `GET /public/v1/plans`; MOD-15 renders W12 and W22 at build time, so a price or cap
change reaches the site on the next publish [WEB W22]. Offer states stay hidden [OD-01].

## 7. Mobile: what it adapts and uses

| Flow            | Screens in order                                                                    | States                                                                                             |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Premium         | D15 (Premium) → P1–P4 (MOD-04, only steps not done) → P5 → P6 → back to the trigger | P5: ready, store sheet open, pending, declined, paid-but-not-confirmed (poll `GET /v1/me/billing`) |

- **Gating and upsell.** Every lock elsewhere routes to D15 with the plan pre-selected from the
  `upsell` value [TR §8.1]. An unverified member goes to B0 first [UF §9].
- **Store actions.** Change and cancel for store plans open the store; refunds follow rule 13 (iOS
  members are guided to Apple); "Restore purchases" calls the API [TR §4.6].
- **Offline.** Purchases and plan changes are never queued; they fail visibly with a retry. BL1 and
  BL2 show the last loaded data with the offline banner (MOD-00 ES5).
- **Restricted and suspended.** Billing screens stay readable; the plan is paused while suspended
  [UF §5.2].
- **Accessibility.** Plan choice is a radio group with the price read as "2 dollars 50 cents a month";
  tier colour is never the only signal; 44 px targets; pay-state changes are announced [TR §2.4].
- **Phone and tablet.** D15 cards sit side by side on tablet and stack on phones; BL6 grids reflow.
  Follow [1MM-STD-03](../standards/1MM-STD-03-MOBILE-BUILD-STRATEGY.md).
