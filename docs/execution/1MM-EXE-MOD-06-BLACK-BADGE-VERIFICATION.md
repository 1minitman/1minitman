**What this document is for and when to open it.** It plans the Black Badge: the member flow B0 to B11, the
verification status screen PM4, the deferred carrier check GD1 and the admin review queue A12. Open it before
you touch anything under `/v1/badge`, `/v1/verification` or `/admin/v1/badges`, and when you change how a
member becomes, stays or stops being verified.

## 1. Outcome

- **Member.** A Free member becomes verified in under 7 minutes of effort and gets a decision within 24 hours,
  with 80% decided automatically within 5 minutes [TR §15.1], [UF §8]. The flow autosaves and resumes. The
  action that sent the member to B0 (for example the Like for one spec) completes by itself on approval
  [UF §8]. A fixable problem costs one redone step and no second payment. A refusal is refunded unless it
  is fraud [TR §2.1]. The member can see what is held about their face and withdraw consent (PM4)
  [TR §16.2].
- **Admin.** Reviewers work one queue ordered by SLA, cannot collide on a case, see scores and results but
  never the liveness video, and decide approve, needs fix or not approved with every decision audited
  [ADM A12].
- **Business.** One person holds one account (phone and face de-duplication) [TR §13.1]. Biometric data is
  processed only with explicit consent and deleted on schedule [TR §16.1]. The Black Badge is a one-off
  $1.00 product that never renews and never lapses [TR §2.1].

## 2. Scope

**In scope**

- Member API: `/v1/badge*`, `/v1/verification/phone/*`, `/v1/verification/liveness/*`,
  `/v1/me/verification`, `DELETE /v1/me/verification/face` [TR §8.2]; and `PUT /v1/me/vibes` (B7),
  which ruling R3 in the [master plan §7](1MM-EXE-00-MASTER-PLAN.md#7-coordinator-rulings) gives to this module.
- Worker jobs `badge.autodecide`, `badge.reminders`, `badge.refund` (orchestration), `liveness.purge`
  [TR §11].
- Admin module A12: queue, review screen, QA sample [ADM A12].
- Mobile screens B0–B11 (B9: the review and submit part) and PM4 [INV].
- Pending-action capture and replay for the `black_badge` gate (`pending_actions`) [DD §4].
- The member side of forced re-verification (a new application at `face_check`, no charge) [ADM A11, AD-13].

### 4.3 Rules the API must enforce

1. The Black Badge is a product, not a plan: no renew, cancel or lapse path exists for it [TR §2.1].
2. Payment comes before review: `submitted_at` cannot be set without `payment_transaction_id` (database
   CHECK) [SQL], [UF D24].
3. One open application per member (`uq_badge_open`) [SQL].
4. Every step autosaves; `GET /v1/badge` returns the resume point [UF §8].
5. One account per phone number; VoIP and virtual numbers are refused; numbers are E.164 [TR §8.2], [UF §8].
6. Codes: 6 digits, valid 10 minutes, resend after 60 s by SMS, WhatsApp or voice, 5 sends an hour; 3 wrong
   tries then a 30-minute lock [TR §13.2]. Termii first, Twilio fallback [UF §8].
7. Liveness is refused without a current `biometric` consent recorded with document version and time
   [TR §16.2].
8. Three liveness tries (`badge.liveness_attempts`), then manual review (`sent_to_manual_review`) [TR §8.2].
9. The API fetches the liveness result server to server; a result reported by the client is never trusted
   [TR §12], [UF §11.2].
10. The face template is used only for duplicate, ban and photo face-match checks [DD §4]. A face
    fingerprint that matches a live or ban-retained template raises a duplicate signal [TR §13.1].
11. `PENDING_REVIEW` keeps Free unverified access (25 specs, no Like) while staged media follows the
    `PENDING_REVIEW` entitlement values [TR §9].
12. `sla_due_at = submitted_at + badge.sla_hours` [ADM A12].
13. Approve: status, `decided_at`, reviewer; badge grant; `users.is_verified` and `verified_at`; role to
    `ACTIVE` or higher with a live plan; pending action replayed; case decided `approve_badge`; N12
    [ADM A12].
14. Needs fix: exactly one `fix_step`, never `pay`; reason code and member message key required; no new
    charge; N13 and a 24 h reminder; the member redoes only that step [ADM A12], [UF §5.2].
15. Not approved, not fraud: `badge.refund` creates the refund and sets `refund_id`; role returns to
    `BASIC`; N29 [ADM A12], [UF §5.3].
16. Not approved, fraud: no refund; second confirmation "No refund will be issued"; "Also ban" follows A11
    and its approval [ADM A12].
17. Two reviewers cannot hold the same application; a claim idle for `cases.claim_timeout_minutes` is
    released [ADM A12].
18. A decision is refused while a vendor result is pending or the payment is missing [ADM A12].
19. The liveness video is never served to any client, including the admin console [ADM A12].
20. Liveness video deleted after the decision, 30 days at most, at the vendor and with us [TR §16.1].
21. Withdrawing biometric consent deletes the template, removes verified, blocks new uploads and does not
    cancel billing; it needs re-authentication [TR §16.2], [TR §8.2]. Phone sign-in follows MOD-03 rules.
22. Pending action: one open per `(user, gate)`; expires after 30 days; replayed once on approval through
    the normal endpoint (for a Like, `POST /v1/reactions` in MOD-07) [SQL], [UF §8].
23. Forced re-verification opens a new application at `face_check` with no charge [ADM A11, AD-13]
    (mechanics: GAP-MOD06-04).
24. Logs and crash reports never carry codes, face data or tokens [TR §14].
25. While `verification.carrier_check` is off no carrier row is written and GD1 is unreachable [DD §4].
