**What this document is for and when to open it.** It plans My Specs (the deck, filters, status, the full
spec profile), reactions with Undo and Rewind, Likes, the match and maybe-match moments, daily caps,
Spotlight and the admin engagement screens (A24). Open it before you touch `/v1/discovery`, `/v1/reactions`,
`/v1/likes`, `/v1/specs`, `/v1/spotlight`, the functions `record_reaction()` and `spend_spec_view()`, or the
A24 tuning and Spotlight screens.

## 1. Outcome

- **Member.** My Specs shows real, nearby, compatible people, fast: the next card is already there, and a
  swipe never waits on the network [TR §15.1]. Pass is safe (6 s Undo). Limits are honest: a visible daily
  counter, a clear screen at the cap, a scarcity card with real numbers and a fix [TR §2.3]. Like and
  Somewhat work for verified members; two reactions that meet create exactly one match or maybe match
  [TR §8.4]. Premium and Gold members get a Spotlight turn in each of three weekly rotations, Gold first
  [OD-18].
- **Admin.** Engagement staff tune caps, timers and thresholds without a release, see and manage Spotlight
  turns, and every change is audited and, in production, approved [ADM A24].
- **Business.** The swipe ledger has no duplicates and no races [TR §3]. Quotas are enforced on the server.
  The discovery deck meets its latency budget [TR §15.1].

## 2. Scope

**In scope**

- Member API group "Discovery, likes, Spotlight" [TR §8.2].
- First-consumer endpoints from the profile group that only this module's screens use:
  `PUT /v1/me/status` (D2) and `PUT /v1/me/likes-filter` (D7F) [TR §8.2].

### 4.3 Rules the API must enforce

1. Reactions are written only through `record_reaction()`, in one transaction, in the order of [TR §8.4]:
   replay check, role check, block check, pair advisory lock, upsert, read the other side, insert the match
   through the partial unique index, create the intro session.
2. A replay of the same `clientId` returns the original outcome and writes nothing [TR §8.4].
3. Like and Somewhat need role `ACTIVE` or higher; Pass is open to everyone [TR §8.4]. A refused Like or
   Somewhat stores a pending action (`like` or `somewhat`, gate `black_badge`) [UF §7.2].
4. No reaction when either person has blocked the other [TR §8.4].
5. Like + Like is a match; Like + Somewhat or Somewhat + Somewhat is a maybe; a later Like on both sides
   upgrades a maybe to a match (function behaviour) [TR §8.4], [SQL].
6. Exactly one active match per pair, under any concurrency [TR §13.1].
7. `intro_expires_at = now + intro.window_hours` for matches and maybe matches [OD-02].
8. Pass sets `undo_until = now + discovery.pass_undo_seconds` and a pair cooldown of
   `discovery.pass_memory_days`; a second Pass on the same person hides them for good [UF §7.3, D20]
   (`reactions.pass_count`; query wording: GAP-MOD07-01).
9. Undo works only inside the window; Rewind is Gold and works at any time; both remove the cooldown and
   log to `reaction_history` [SQL].
10. Daily cap: 25 for `BASIC` and `PENDING_REVIEW`, 50 for `ACTIVE`, none for `CLASSIC` and `SUPER`,
    counted on the server per member local day through `spend_spec_view()` with the row lock; Redis is only
    the fast path [TR §8.5], [OD-03]. The local day comes from `profiles.timezone`.
11. At the cap the deck answers `402 quota_exhausted` with the upsell: Black Badge for unverified, Premium
    for verified Free [TR §2.3].
12. Discovery candidates follow [TR §8.5]: active and not hidden; inside the distance (or in the Gold
    member's chosen countries); age, gender and interest matching both ways; intentions; `verified_only`
    and `active_this_week`; deal-breakers for Premium+; not blocked either way; not hidden by the viewer;
    no live pair cooldown; no live Like or Somewhat by the viewer; a passed person excluded only
    while the Pass cooldown runs or once `reactions.pass_count >= 2`; Incognito members only where
    they liked the viewer (TRX-040); restricted accounts excluded.
13. Order: Spotlight priority, shared-spec score, recently active, distance; 50 candidates cached, 10
    served, refilled under 15; no repeat within a day (`feed:served:*`) [TR §8.5], [DD §14].
14. An Incognito member appears only to people they liked [TR §9], [OD-34]. An Invisible member is never
    published as present [DD §14].
15. Exact locations are never returned: distance bands from coarse points only [TR §16.2].
16. The scarcity payload uses real counts only and appears when the city's new verified members this week
    are below `discovery.scarcity_threshold` [TR §8.5], [OD-31].
17. International: Gold only, at most 5 countries; while no other market is open the tab returns "Opening
    soon" with a waitlist count, never an empty list [OD-09].
18. A full profile needs the Black Badge; fields are shaped by the viewer's role: Free unverified sees
    main photo, age, intention; verified adds photos, clip, interests, bio, prompts; Premium and Gold add
    lifestyle, career, partner preferences and relationship history [UF §10.1].
19. Likes received: Free unverified gets a count and blurred images only; the API never sends identities
    or sharp image URLs to a member without `likes.see_who` [TR §9]. **Recommendation:** blurred variants
    are rendered by the media pipeline (MOD-04), not blurred on the device.
20. Likes sort and filter need `likes.sort_filter` [OD-31]. `v_likes_received` excludes people the member
    already reacted to [SQL].
21. Reading a likes page sets `reactions.seen_by_target_at`; reading a match sets `seen_by_a_at` or
    `seen_by_b_at`; both clear `badge:{user_id}` (**Recommendation**: no source names the trigger).
22. Hide removes a person from My Specs only; the person is not told; it is reversible (H5) [DD §5].
23. Spotlight: 3 rotations a week; every eligible verified Premium and Gold member gets one turn per
    rotation; Gold placed first; restricted accounts and opt-outs excluded; N09 once per turn [OD-18],
    [SQL]. `usage_counters` metric `spotlight_turns` records turns used.
24. Status: Invisible needs `presence.invisible`; the note is at most 40 characters and passes the
    contact-detail filter [TR §8.2], [TR §13.4].
25. Reactions are limited to 120 a minute per member [TR §13.3].
26. The mobile outbox may replay reactions, reads and status changes in order after reconnect; the API
    accepts them idempotently [TR §4.2].
27. Budgets: deck p95 under 300 ms at the origin; hot queries have EXPLAIN checks in CI [TR §15].
