This plan covers how a person becomes a member and proves it is them afterwards: welcome, the
10-step sign-up, Google and Apple, sign-in, new-device confirmation, recovery, phone sign-in, the
under-18 block, sessions, password and security screens, settings, and sign-in to the billing web
app. Open it when you touch any `/v1/auth/*` endpoint, a session or token, an OTP, a lockout, or any
screen listed in §2.

## 1. Outcome

When this module is done:

- **A new member** goes from the welcome screens to My Specs in 10 steps, by email and password or by
  Google or Apple, with progress saved after every screen and resumed on return [TR §2.2], [UF §6].
  A person under 18 is stopped and the device is flagged for 30 days [TR §13.2].
- **A returning member** signs in with email and password, with a linked provider, or (after the
  Black Badge) with their phone; confirms a new device with a one-time code; recovers a forgotten
  password without the service revealing whether an account exists; and is paused after five wrong
  passwords [TR §13.2].
- **A signed-in member** sees their devices and sessions, signs out one or all others, changes their
  password, answers "Was this you?" after a new sign-in, manages sign-in methods and app settings
  [UF §15.18].
- **A member on the web** signs in to the billing web app with the same account and a cookie session
  [TR §6.2].
- **An admin** changes every limit this flow uses (password length, code lifetime, resend wait,
  lockout, rate limits, whether registration is open, whether a beta city needs an invite) from the
  pages MOD-02 built, with no deploy.
- **The business** has one account per person per email, phone and provider id, no silent account
  merges, and an auditable trail of sign-ins, lockouts and resets.

**Endpoints claimed from other API groups.** This module runs before the owners of the "Me" and
"Legal" groups, and API-first forbids a sign-up screen without its endpoint. It therefore builds
`GET /v1/me` (identity fields), `PATCH /v1/me/onboarding`, `POST /v1/me/onboarding/complete`,
`PUT /v1/me/nickname`, `PUT /v1/me/location`, `GET / PUT /v1/me/settings`, `POST /v1/consents` and the
read `GET /v1/legal/{kind}?locale` [TR §8.2]. It also delivers the read-only entitlement resolver
and `GET /v1/me/entitlements`, which resolve from the seeded `role_entitlements` and the member's
role [TR §9]; [MOD-10](1MM-EXE-MOD-10-BILLING-PLANS-AND-ENTITLEMENTS.md) owns everything that
changes entitlements (rulings R2 and R12 in the [master plan §7](1MM-EXE-00-MASTER-PLAN.md#7-coordinator-rulings)). MOD-04 extends `GET /v1/me` and reuses the nickname
write; [MOD-16](1MM-EXE-MOD-16-LEGAL-PRIVACY-AND-DATA-OPERATIONS.md) owns the legal documents
themselves, A30, LG1–LG3 and consent history.

### 4.3 Rules the API must enforce

1. Every account has email and password, except a provider-only member, who has no `password_credentials` row until they add one in Settings › Sign-in methods [TR §13.2], [SQL].
2. Email is trimmed and lower-cased; one account per email while not deleted (`uq_users_email`) [SQL].
3. Disposable email domains are refused at E1 [UF §6.3].
4. Reset and login never reveal whether an email has an account: same response and comparable timing [TR §13.2].
5. Passwords: at least `auth.password_min_length` characters (8), checked against breached-password lists by k-anonymity, hashed with Argon2id [TR §13.2], [OD-07].
6. Codes: 6 digits, valid `otp.ttl_minutes` (10; the table refuses more than 10 minutes), resend after `otp.resend_seconds` (60), at most 5 sends an hour, stored hashed [TR §13.2], [SQL].
7. Wrong codes: SMS, WhatsApp or voice 3 tries then a 30-minute lock; email 5 tries then a 15-minute wait [TR §13.2].
8. Five wrong passwords for an account lead to the X1 15-minute pause; 50 attempts per 15 minutes per IP [TR §13.3]. A lock is written to Redis and to `account_lockouts` [DD §14].
9. Sign-up: at most 5 a day per device and 20 an hour per IP [TR §13.3].
10. Play Integrity / App Attest verdict is checked at sign-up and login and stored on the device row; no CAPTCHAs [TR §13.2].
11. A sign-in from an unseen fingerprint needs a code by SMS, WhatsApp, voice or email (L2 / L3) and sends an alert [TR §13.2].
12. PM6 "It wasn't me" revokes every session, forces a password reset (`password_resets.source = not_me`, `must_change`) and opens a fraud review [TR §13.2], [SQL].
13. Refresh tokens rotate; reuse of a used token revokes the whole family (`revoke_reason = token_reuse`) [TR §8.1].
14. Access token: JWT (EdDSA), 15 minutes. Refresh token: 30 days, stored as SHA-256 [TR §8.1].
15. Phone sign-in works only when the number is verified and `sign_in_enabled`; it turns off on a number change or a detected SIM swap [TR §13.2], [SQL].
16. Google and Apple: match by provider `sub`; Apple private-relay emails match by `sub` only. An email match with an unlinked account requires S2 (password or email code); nothing is linked or merged silently; the member is emailed after linking [CN], [SQL].
17. A member cannot remove their last sign-in method [TR §13.2].
18. Never create a duplicate account: a provider id or verified email that matches an existing member signs that member in (OB4) and resumes at `onboarding_step` if set-up is unfinished [UF §6.4].
19. Onboarding saves after every screen and resumes at the first incomplete step; Google and Apple paths start at step 5 [TR §5.3], [UF §6.6].
20. Age: 18 or over on the day; the birth date is locked after the confirm sheet; under 18 → no account is kept (the `onboarding` user row and its sessions are deleted in the same transaction, TRX-060), device hash blocked for 30 days, date of birth not stored [TR §13.2], [SQL].
21. C5: all three boxes are required; each acceptance stores kind, document version and time [UF §6.5].
22. C6: location is snapped to a ~1 km grid before storing; exact location is never stored [SQL].
23. Sign-up in a `beta` city redeems an invite through `redeem_beta_invite()` while `markets.beta_requires_invite` is true [TR §6.1].
24. First and last name are never shown to other members; the nickname defaults to the first name; names and nicknames pass the reserved-name and contact-detail rules [UF §6.3], [SQL].
25. Sensitive endpoints (password change, sign-in methods, export, deletion) need re-authentication in the last 5 minutes [TR §8.2].
26. A sign-in during `deletion_pending` returns that status so the app shows AS5 before home; `suspended` and `banned` return their status so the app shows H6; what such a session may do (appeal, export) is set by MOD-05 [UF §15.2], [UF §5.2].
27. Passwords, codes and tokens are never logged [TR §14].
28. Web app: the same accounts, an HttpOnly secure cookie session, and L2 / L3 on a new device [TR §6.2].
