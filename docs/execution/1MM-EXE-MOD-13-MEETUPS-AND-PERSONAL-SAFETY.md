This is the execution plan for planning a meet-up in a public place, the date safety check-in and
its escalation, trusted contacts and the public page they receive, the in-app safety centre, the
admin safety desk, and the events and venues part of the admin Groups module. Open it before
building any endpoint, job, webhook handler, page or screen in that area. A missed check-in is the
most time-critical path in the product: read sections 4.2 and 4.3 before changing any of it.

## 1. Outcome

| For               | What is true when this module is done                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ----------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A member          | From a chat, a member plans a date at a curated public venue, never a home or a hotel [OD-30]; the other person accepts or suggests another time within 48 hours [UF §15.22]. A group admin plans a group event [TR §8.2]. At the planned end of a date the member gets a check-in; with no answer in 15 minutes their trusted contacts and the safety desk are alerted with the last coarse area [TR §11]. A member keeps up to 3 trusted contacts, each active only after replying YES [TR §16.2]. The safety centre shows the emergency number for the member's country: Nigeria 112 only at launch [OD-09]. |
| A trusted contact | Receives one opt-in message, then, per date, a link to `https://1minitman.com/s/<token>` that needs no login, shows the time, the place and the match's verified card, and expires 24 hours after the meet-up [OD-13], [CN legal-rules]. STOP ends it.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| An admin          | Staff with `safety.escalations` see every escalation within seconds, acknowledge inside 15 minutes, reveal a number or a coarse area only with a reason, and record each step on an append-only timeline [ADM A15]. Staff with `groups.moderate` cancel group events; staff with `venues.manage` keep the venue catalogue [ADM A18].                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| The business      | Third-party consent, location minimisation and the retention limits of [TR §16] are enforced by the server and the worker. Every timer and limit is configuration (section 3.4).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |

## 2. Scope

**In scope**

- Member API group "Meet-ups and safety" of [TR §8.2]: venues, meet-ups (create, read, RSVP, proposals, cancel, check-in, snooze, feedback), trusted contacts, `POST /v1/safety/help`, and `GET /public/v1/share/{token}`.
- Inbound YES and STOP replies from trusted contacts on the SMS and WhatsApp provider webhooks [TR §12].
- Jobs `meetup.reminders`, `checkin.due`, `checkin.escalate`, `share_links.expire`, `safety.location.purge` [TR §11].

### 4.3 Rules the API must enforce

1. A meet-up always has a venue from `venues`; there is no free-text address field anywhere in the contract. Venues are always public places, never homes or hotels [OD-30], [SQL].
2. A date belongs to exactly one conversation and a group event to exactly one group; capacity is set only on group events [SQL].
3. A date can be planned only by a participant of an open conversation; a group event only by the group's owner or an admin-role member [TR §8.2].
4. `ends_at` defaults to `starts_at` plus `meetup.default_duration_minutes`; `ends_at > starts_at` [SQL], [DDR M39].
5. A date invite sets `invite_expires_at` = now + `meetup.invite_expiry_hours` and posts a `date_invite` message in the chat; it expires unanswered after that time [TR §11], [CN meetup-rules].
6. The other person accepts or suggests another time; a suggestion sets `new_time_suggested`, and accepting a suggestion updates `starts_at` / `ends_at` and supersedes other proposals [SQL], [UF §15.22].
7. Accepting records `going` and whether the member added the event to the device calendar [SQL].
8. Group events: verified members only by default (`join_rule = 'verified_only'`); capacity counts `going` only; no payment is collected and `cost_note` is text [CN meetup-rules], [DDR M7].
9. When a date is accepted, a `safety_checkins` row exists for each participant with `due_at` = `ends_at` and `respond_by` = `due_at` + 15 minutes; a time change moves both; a cancel sets them `cancelled` [SQL], [TR §11]. (Whether both participants or only the planner get a check-in is confirmed under GAP-MOD13-03.)
10. An `ok` answer closes the check-in. "Add 1 hour" moves `due_at` and `respond_by` forward one hour and increments `snooze_count`, at most 6 times [SQL].
11. No answer by `respond_by`: trusted contacts and the safety queue are alerted with the last coarse area [TR §11].
12. "I need help" raises a `need_help` escalation at once, without waiting for `respond_by` [TR §8.2], [ADM A15].
13. A member has at most `trusted_contacts.max` live contacts (database ceiling 3) and no duplicate number [SQL].
14. A new contact gets exactly one opt-in message and stays `waiting_opt_in` until they reply YES; nothing about any date is sent to a contact who is not `active` [TR §16.2], [CN legal-rules].
15. STOP sets `opted_out`, sends one stop confirmation and revokes that contact's live share links [CN legal-rules], [SQL].
16. Sharing: when a date is accepted and the member chose to share, each active contact gets a `meetup_shares` row and a `date_shared` message. Only the SHA-256 hash of the token is stored; the token appears only in the message [SQL].
17. A share link expires `share_link.expiry_hours_after` (24) hours after the meet-up ends [TR §8.2].
18. The share response contains only the time, the place and the match's verified card; after a missed check-in it adds the last coarse area and the member's number. It never contains chat content, the match's phone number or an exact location [CN legal-rules], [TR §16.2].
19. The share endpoint sends `Cache-Control: no-store` [WEB §2.2] and answers unknown, expired and revoked tokens identically (**Recommendation**, so a token cannot be probed).
20. Location is coarse (about 1 km) wherever it is stored or shown [TR §16.2]; it is deleted 30 days after the meet-up [TR §16.1].
21. The emergency number shown is `countries.emergency_number` for the member's country; where it is NULL no number is shown; where it is not verified the app says so [OD-09], [SQL comment].
22. Feedback is private. `noshow` writes a `no_show` trust signal and `bad` a `didnt_feel_right` signal against the other participant; `bad` leads to H3 [CN meetup-rules], [SQL].
23. Escalation writes: acknowledge, contacted member, contacted authorities (who, when, optional reference), resolve (outcome and note) and false alarm (note) each append one immutable update row [SQL], [ADM A15].
24. A reveal needs `safety.escalations` and a reason, is audited per kind, appends a reveal row, and is refused once the escalation is resolved or a false alarm [ADM A15].
25. Date meet-ups never appear in `/admin/v1/events`; they are visible to staff only through an escalation [ADM A18].
26. An admin event cancel stores `cancelled_by_kind = 'admin'`, the admin, a reason code and an optional note, and notifies members [ADM A18], [DDR A34].
27. Venue writes cannot set `is_public_place` to false; the contract has no such field [ADM A18], [OD-30].
28. Phone numbers, tokens and locations never appear in logs, traces or analytics [TR §14], [TR §13.4].
29. A blocked pair cannot plan or keep a date: MOD-09's block calls this module's `cancelMeetupsForPair` service (**Recommendation**; see Q2).
