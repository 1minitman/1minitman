This is the execution plan for the legal documents members and visitors read, the consent ledger,
data export, account deletion and erasure, the retention jobs, the admin legal and privacy
modules and the website consent manager. Open it before building any of those, and whenever
another module adds a table that holds personal data: its retention rule and its place in the
export and the erasure are registered here.

## 1. Outcome

- **For a member:** LG1, LG2 and LG3 show the published version in their language with a short
  version of at most 600 characters [OD-36]. LG6 lets them download their data once a day, read
  their consent history, withdraw sensitive-data consent, reach the DPO and start deletion. AS2 to
  AS4 schedule deletion with a 14-day grace, and AS5 lets them keep the account [UF §15.16],
  [UF §15.23]. A material change to a document is announced 14 days ahead and must be accepted
  [TR §16.2], [ADM App. D AD-40].
- **For an admin:** A30 versions, approves and publishes legal documents and shows acceptance
  progress. A36 is the DPO register with due dates, exports, deletions with "Erase now" under
  approval, the consent audit, retention compliance checks, biometrics counts and legal holds
  [ADM A30], [ADM A36].
- **For a visitor:** the website loads no analytics or marketing script before an explicit choice
  (W03), and every legal document is readable with its version history (W35) [WEB §3.10].
- **For the business:** each retention rule of [TR §16.1] has a job, a run record and a check that
  reads the real tables; consent is provable per member, kind and version; the NDPA 2023 / GDPR
  duties of [TR §16.2] are met by working software, not by policy text alone.

## 2. Scope

**In scope**

1. Legal authoring and publishing (A30), the member read of versions, re-acceptance.
2. The consent ledger read side (`GET /v1/me/consents`, A36 Consents) and sensitive-data
   withdrawal (`PUT /v1/me/privacy/sensitive`).
3. Data export (`/v1/me/export`, `export.build`, `exports.purge`).
4. Account deletion and erasure (`/v1/me/deletion`, `deletion.remind`, `erasure.run`, "Erase now").
5. Retention: `retention.purge`, `sensitive.erase`, the A36 Retention tab and its compliance checks,
   and the registry that maps every [TR §16.1] row to a job.
6. A36 in full: requests register, exports, deletions, consents, retention, biometrics, legal holds.
7. Website W03 (consent manager, receipts) and W35 (legal pages, version history).
8. Mobile LG1, LG2, LG3, LG6, AS2, AS3, AS4, AS5.

### 4.3 Rules the API must enforce

1. A published legal document cannot be edited or returned to draft; a change is a new version
   [SQL `legal_published_immutable()`], [ADM A30].
2. The short version is at most 600 characters [OD-36], [SQL `ck_legal_short`].
3. Publishing needs `legal.publish`, a reason, a second admin's approval and re-auth [TR §7.1],
   [ADM App. A.7]. The requester cannot approve.
4. Terms, Privacy, Guidelines, the biometric notice and offer terms cannot be published without
   `counsel_review_ref` [SQL `ck_legal_counsel_ref`], [OD-10].
5. The entity-line validator passes before a publish request is accepted; the published Privacy
   Policy names the interim DPO and the NDPC registration [ADM A30], [TR §16.2].
6. All three locales exist, or the missing ones are marked `fallback_to_en`, before publishing; a
   pcm or fr row needs a native review [ADM A30], [SQL `content_workflow_guard()`].
7. For a material change, `reaccept_from` is at least 14 days after publish [SQL
   `ck_legal_reaccept`], [TR §16.2].
8. From `reaccept_from` the member is asked to accept the new version; after `effective_at` a
   member who has not accepted sees a blocking re-accept screen on next open, and nothing else
   about the account changes [ADM App. D AD-40]. Delivery of this state: GAP-MOD16-02.
9. Acceptance writes a new `consent_records` row with `document_id`, `version` and `screen_code`;
   rows are never updated or deleted [SQL], [TR §16.2].
10. Withdrawing genotype or faith consent writes a `withdrawn` row, opens a `privacy_requests`
    row of kind `erase_sensitive` with `source = 'app'`, and the answers are erased within 30 days
    [CN legal-rules], [TR §16.1], [ADM A36].
11. Export: once a day per member (`UNIQUE (user_id, requested_on)`, policy `export.user`),
    re-auth within 5 minutes, link valid `export.link_valid_days` and never more than 7 days,
    file deleted at expiry, row deleted at 30 days [TR §8.2], [TR §13.3], [TR §16.1], [SQL].
12. The ZIP holds profile, photos, messages, matches and payments [TR §11]; it never holds
    another member's private data beyond the messages the member received, face-template data or
    internal risk scores (**Recommendation**, to be confirmed in the lawyer review [OD-10]).
13. Deletion needs re-auth by password, Google or Apple and the typed confirmation
    [UF §15.16]; `reauth_method` is recorded [SQL].
14. On `POST /v1/me/deletion`, in one transaction: status becomes `deletion_pending`, the profile
    is hidden at once, every session is revoked, N20 is queued with the undo link
    [CN account-rules], [SQL].
15. One scheduled deletion per member [SQL `uq_deletion_scheduled`]; `scheduled_for` is
    `requested_at` + 14 days [SQL].
16. Store subscriptions cannot be cancelled by the server: AS2 warns and links to the store and
    `store_cancel_warned` is set; a web (Flutterwave) plan is cancelled automatically and
    `web_billing_cancelled_at` is set [UF §15.16], [SQL].
17. Any successful sign-in during `deletion_pending` reaches AS5 before home; Keep cancels the
    deletion and restores the previous status; Continue signs out [CN account-rules].
18. Erasure keeps payment records for 7 years with the member pseudonymised, keeps consent
    records for account + 6 years, keeps the `account_deletions` row as pseudonymised proof, and
    keeps a one-way face fingerprint only for banned accounts (`keep_face_fingerprint`)
    [TR §16.1], [SQL].
19. `erasure.run` and every purge skip any subject for which `has_legal_hold()` is true, including
    a hold still waiting for approval, and log the skip [SQL], [ADM A36].
20. "Erase now" needs `users.erase`, a reason, approval and re-auth; it is refused while a hold is
    live [ADM A36], [TR §7.3].
21. Every privacy request gets `due_at` from `privacy.sla_days.<kind>` at creation and a PR-n
    reference; Complete needs a note; Reject needs a reason [ADM A36], [OD-38].
22. A request not made by a signed-in member cannot be completed until `identity_verified_at` is
    set [ADM A36].
23. Publishing a `cookies` document sets `site_settings.consent.policy_version` to its version in
    the same transaction, so returning visitors are asked again [WEB W03], [WEB §5.8].
24. A consent receipt with `gpc = true` has marketing false [SQL]; the receipt never stores an IP
    or user agent [WEB §3.10].
25. A36 never returns a biometric template or a liveness video, only counts [ADM A36].
26. Destructive jobs cannot be run on demand [SQL `worker_jobs` CHECK].
27. Legal-hold placement and release need `privacy.legal_holds` and approval; `review_at` is in
    the future [ADM A36], [SQL].
28. Birth-date rectification is done only from a privacy request, with evidence and approval
    [ADM A36]; the edit itself is MOD-05's command.
29. A website contact submission of kind `data_request` opens a `privacy_requests` row with
    `source = 'web_form'` and `contact_submission_id`; a request received at the privacy mailbox
    is logged by the DPO with `source = 'email'` [ADM A36], [SQL]. The form itself (W33) is MOD-14's, which inserts the row.
30. An `erase_account` request schedules a deletion with `source = 'privacy_request'` and the
    14-day grace, or goes to "Erase now" under approval [ADM A36], [SQL].
