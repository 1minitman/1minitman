This is the execution plan for reporting, blocking, the moderation queue, appeals, the sanctions
ladder, abuse rules, macros and risk signals. Open it before building any of those endpoints,
admin views or screens, and whenever a change touches reported evidence, which is the most
sensitive data path in the product after biometrics.

## 1. Outcome

| For          | What is true when this module is done                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A member     | Can report a person or content in three steps from every entry point; the reported person is never told [UF §15.11]. Can block instantly and silently with an Undo, see and manage the blocked and hidden list, and unblock with an Undo [CN safety-rules]. Hears back when a report is reviewed (N22). Can appeal a restriction, suspension or warning once, within 30 days, and gets a decision within 48 hours [OD-35], [OD-28]. |
| An admin     | Works one queue for every human decision, ordered by priority then due time [OD-27]. Reads evidence only as frozen snapshots, only after a reason and step-up, with every open audited [ADM A14]. Decides appeals without marking their own homework, follows a ladder that suggests the next sanction, and manages abuse rules, macros and risk views [ADM A16], [ADM A17].                                                        |
| The business | Under-18 and threat reports reach a human within 1 hour and everything else within 24 hours [UF §15.11]. Every sanction, evidence view and rule change is attributable for 7 years [TR §16.1].                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |

## 2. Scope

**In scope**

- Member API: `POST /v1/reports`, `GET /v1/me/reports`, `POST /v1/blocks`, `DELETE /v1/blocks/{id}`, `GET /v1/me/blocked`, `GET /v1/me/account-status`, `POST /v1/appeals` [TR §8.2].
- The server side of the chat safety actions: closing the pair's match and conversation on a block, freezing evidence on a report, inline abuse rules, the in-chat warning, trust signals [TR §13.4].
- Admin modules A14 (reports and moderation cases), A16 (appeals and sanctions ladder), A17 (abuse rules, macros, risk signals).
- Mobile screens H3, H4, H5, and the Block and Report entry points on screens owned by other modules.

### 4.3 Rules the API must enforce

**Reports**

1. The reported person is never told who reported them, and no response, notification or timing reveals a report [UF §15.11].
2. A report takes its `severity` and `sla_due_at` from `report_reasons` (`now + sla_minutes`); `harass` and `minor` are due in 60 minutes and notify on creation; `offline` is 24 hours [SQL], [ADM A14].
3. A report from a chat with `include_messages` freezes the last 50 messages into `report_evidence` in the same transaction; a call report freezes time and duration only [UF §15.11], [CN matching-rules].
4. Evidence is a copy: it survives unmatch, block and erasure for 12 months after resolution, unless a legal hold extends it [TR §16.1].
5. Each report creates or joins one `moderation_cases` row (`UNIQUE (kind, source_table, source_id)`), with `priority` from the report's severity (higher is more urgent) and `country` from the subject member [SQL], [DD §13B.3].
6. A member can file 20 reports a day [TR §13.3]; nobody can report themselves (table CHECK).
7. "Block and report" files the report with `also_blocked = true` and creates the block [INV H4].
8. Several money-related reports restrict the account pending review (`account_restrictions.source = 'money_reports'`) [TR §13.4]; the threshold is GAP-MOD09-08.

**Blocks**

9. A block is instant and silent: both people disappear from each other's My Specs, Likes, Groups and DMs, and the chat is removed [CN safety-rules]; `is_blocked_pair()` refuses reactions and messages both ways [TR §8.4].
10. Undo is available for `block.undo_seconds`; an unblock from H5 also has an Undo [UF §15.11]. What Undo restores is settled by TRX-004 (raised as GAP-MOD09-07): the pair is hidden at once, the irreversible close is applied by the worker only after `blocks.undo_until`, and Undo inside the window restores visibility and writes no pair cooldown.
11. Re-matching is possible `block.rematch_hours` (48) after an unblock (`pair_cooldowns.reason = 'unblocked'`) [OD-02], [SQL].
12. Members never see that they were blocked [CN safety-rules].

**Cases and evidence**

13. The queue order is `priority` desc then `sla_due_at` asc; "Claim next" takes the first unclaimed row in that order; a possible-minor or safety case is always worked first [OD-27].
14. A claim with no decision is released after `cases.claim_timeout_minutes` [ADM App. D, AD-26].
15. A queue-scoped reviewer sees only their queue's unclaimed cases and their own claims; a country-scoped admin sees only that country; other rows return 404 [ADM §1.1].
16. Evidence is served only by the evidence endpoint, only from `report_evidence`, only after a reason and a step-up within 5 minutes; every open writes `report.evidence.view`; no admin endpoint returns live `messages` content [ADM A14].
17. The viewer shows a watermark with the admin's display name and the time, and offers no download or copy-all [ADM A14]. **Recommendation:** evidence responses are sent with `Cache-Control: no-store`.
18. Deciding a report updates the case, the report status and resolution, and queues N22 to the reporter [ADM A14].
19. A possible minor can be asked for an age check: the profile is hidden with `hidden_reason = 'age_check'`, the case waits as `awaiting_member`, and a confirmed minor is banned with reason `minor` [OD-33].
20. A ban or erasure decided from a case needs a second admin; the case stays `claimed` until the approval executes [ADM A14], [ADM §1.2].
21. `moderation_actions` is append-only; an overturn is a new row with `reverses_action_id` [SQL].

**Appeals and the ladder**

22. One appeal per decision; the target is exactly one of a restriction or a warning (`ck_appeal_target`) [OD-35].
23. An appeal must be filed within `appeals.window_days` (30) of the decision and is decided within `appeals.sla_hours` (48) [OD-35], [OD-28].
24. The admin who made the original decision cannot decide its appeal [ADM A16].
25. Overturning lifts the sanction and credits the paid days that overlapped; overturning a ban goes through the unban approval [ADM A16].
26. The ladder suggests the next step from the member's `moderation_actions` in the category's look-back window; deviating needs `off_ladder_reason` [ADM A16], [SQL].
27. Ladder steps use only warn, restrict, suspend and ban; `minor` always ends in ban; edits are validated and approved under `sanctions.manage` [DD §13B.3].

**Abuse rules and risk**

28. Rule create, edit and disable need approval and bump `version` [ADM A17].
29. Regex patterns are compiled with a timeout and refused when they fail the complexity check [ADM A17].
30. `auto_restrict` is allowed only for `velocity` and `classifier` rules [ADM A17].
31. Hits and failures never store the matched text; the test panel never stores its sample [ADM A17].
32. The contact-detail filter covers nicknames, names, bios, job titles, group names and DM notes [TR §13.4].
33. Trust signals are never shown to members [SQL].
